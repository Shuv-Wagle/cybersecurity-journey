# Day 01 — ICMP & Ping Sweeps

## What I learned

- ICMP stands for Internet Control Message Protocol.
- ICMP is used for network diagnostics and communication.
- Ping uses ICMP to check whether a device is reachable.
- Traceroute can show the routers/hops between two systems.
- ICMP Timestamp Requests can be used to request the time from another device.
- A Ping Sweep checks multiple IP addresses to find devices that respond.
- A device not responding to ping does not always mean it is offline because ICMP may be blocked.

## Practice

I studied how Ping, Traceroute, and Ping Sweeps use ICMP.

## Key takeaway

ICMP helps us understand whether devices are reachable and troubleshoot networks.
